Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information AuraMind AI collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

AuraMind AI is an AI automation agency based in Lefkoşa, Northern Cyprus. You can reach us at hello@aiauramind.com or on WhatsApp at +1 (573) 409-5760.

What we collect

  • Contact form details. If you use the consultation form, you give us your name, business name, business type, what you would like to automate, and your WhatsApp number.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us on WhatsApp, by email, or try the demo assistant, we receive whatever you write.
  • Language preference. Your browser stores your chosen language (English, Turkish or Arabic) so the site remembers it. This stays on your device.

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels.

How the contact form works

The form has no database behind it. When you send it, your details are placed in a WhatsApp message and WhatsApp opens so you can send it to us. The message is then handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss whether we are a good fit.
  • To prepare proposals and set up services you ask us for.
  • To keep in touch about work we are doing for you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • WhatsApp (Meta). Carries the messages you send to us.
  • Google Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google, so your browser contacts Google servers and your IP address is visible to them.
  • Our hosting provider. Serves the website and may keep standard server logs.

Where we build an assistant for a client, the conversations that assistant handles are covered by the agreement we make with that client.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@aiauramind.com and we will respond. You can clear your language preference at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
